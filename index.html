❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is Royal Panel Chart?
Royal Panel Chart is a live updated chart showing daily Matka results for all days of the week. We provide accurate and up-to-date records.
How often is the chart updated?
The chart is updated live as soon as new results are available. You can see the LIVE badge at the top indicating real-time updates.
Is the data accurate?
Yes, our data is sourced directly from official records and updated regularly. However, this website is for entertainment purposes only.
Can I download the chart?
Yes! Use the PDF button in the quick actions to download the chart, or use the Print option to save it as a PDF.
How do I contact support?
Use the contact form below or click on the WhatsApp floating button to reach us directly.
